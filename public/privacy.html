01. Overview

Piko Label Printer is a private shipping workflow tool operated for the Piko Cards eBay store. It is intended for the store owner's use to prepare orders, not as a consumer-facing eBay marketplace or advertising platform.

This policy covers information processed through Piko Label Printer. eBay's own services are subject to eBay's separate privacy notices.

02. Information we access

After the seller authorizes access, Piko Label Printer requests order information from eBay's Sell Fulfillment API, including:

  • Buyer names, usernames and shipping addresses
  • Order identifiers, dates and fulfillment status
  • Items purchased, quantities and relevant prices or shipping amounts

The application also processes the return address and print-format preferences entered by the store owner.

03. How information is used

We use order details only to show orders requiring fulfillment and generate shipping address labels and packing slips. The connected eBay permission is read-only for order access. Piko Label Printer does not use buyer information for advertising or sell it.

04. Storage and security

In the current version, Piko Label Printer does not maintain its own persistent database of eBay buyers or orders. Order information is requested when needed; PDF generation is performed by server-side functions and returned to the browser.

The application uses encrypted, HTTP-only browser cookies for its eBay connection and application session. Business and printing preferences are saved locally in the browser on the device. Customer order details are not intentionally saved in that preference storage.

Printing and downloads: Generated PDFs may contain personal shipping details. If a document is downloaded, printed or shared through a device's print service, copies may remain on that device, printer or service until removed by the operator.

The application is hosted on Vercel. Operational logs and technical metadata may be handled by the hosting provider in accordance with its service settings and policies. Access to the app should be restricted using its configured password.

05. Service providers and printing

Information is exchanged with eBay to retrieve authorized orders and processed through Vercel-hosted application functions. When the user opens, downloads or prints a generated document, the browser, Android printing system, printer or other selected print service may process its contents.

We do not intentionally provide buyer order information to unrelated marketing companies or data brokers.

06. Access, retention and control

The store owner can disconnect eBay in Piko Label Printer's Settings, which clears the application's local eBay authorization cookie. Access may also be revoked from the seller's eBay account. Disconnecting the application is not the same as deleting downloaded files or previously printed labels.

Printing preferences and business details can be removed by clearing the site's browser storage. Any locally downloaded PDFs or printouts containing addresses should be kept only as needed for shipping and disposed of securely. Hosting-provider logs may have separate retention periods.

Questions or requests related to customer shipping information can be directed to Piko Cards through the eBay store's messaging channel. Requests concerning eBay's own account data should be directed to eBay.

07. Contact

For privacy questions about this application, contact the operator, Piko Cards, through its eBay store.
