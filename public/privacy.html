Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Piko Label Printer is a private shipping-workflow tool used by the Piko Cards eBay seller account. This policy explains how the tool handles order information.

Information accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the application uses the eBay Sell Fulfillment API to access unfulfilled order information, including buyer names, shipping addresses, order identifiers, purchased items, prices and shipping information.

How this information is used

Order information is used to display orders to prepare and to generate address-label and packing-slip PDFs. Order information is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

Storage and security

The app does not maintain a separate customer-order database. Order data is requested from eBay as needed and processed by server-side functions to generate PDFs. PDFs are returned to the browser for viewing, downloading or printing. The app uses encrypted HTTP-only cookies to keep eBay authorization between visits. Printing preferences and sender details are saved in browser storage on the device.

Hosting and application processing use Vercel infrastructure. Standard service logs and platform security measures may apply. Protect any downloaded or printed labels because they contain customer addresses.

Data sharing

Information is provided to eBay and the infrastructure required to operate the application. When a user chooses to print or download a PDF, the destination printer, print service and device may process that document. The app does not intentionally distribute buyer details to unrelated parties.

Controls and retention

The owner can disconnect eBay in the application's Settings to delete the app's local authorization cookie, and may revoke access through the eBay account's third-party application settings. Browser-stored preferences can be cleared using the browser's site-data controls. Downloaded PDFs must be deleted by the person who downloaded them. Separate hosting-service logs are governed by the provider's policies.

Contact

For questions about this application's handling of shipping data, contact Piko Cards through its official eBay store messaging channel.
